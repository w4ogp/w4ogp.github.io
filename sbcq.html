Skippy's Beer Can War
UNEF · Unauthorized AI Collaboration

Skippy's Beer Can War is a fan action game inspired by the Expeditionary Force books. You command a UNEF squad (or dropship) stranded in a hostile alien hierarchy—Kristang warriors, Thuranin sensors, Maxolhx apex ships—armed with almost nothing except Skippy, an ancient Elder AI in a beer can who can hack fleets, override your controls, and trash-talk you the whole time. Complete escalating missions, collect Elder tech, unlock Skippy's dangerous abilities, steal a Maxolhx starship, and jump home… if his "help" doesn't get you permanently killed first.

Needs a real keyboard and mouse. Too awesome for your filthy iPhone.
